Three constructions

Pick the construction from the artwork

Jacket patches are usually one of three constructions. Chenille and leather read as heritage, embroidery carries fine detail — the artwork decides, not the price.

Embroidered

Highest stitch coverage, so fine detail and small lettering survive. Pilot $0.65/pc, then $0.19–$0.38/pc at volume. Embroidery patches.

Chenille

Raised loop pile for varsity and letterman looks. Pilot $0.65/pc, then $0.25–$0.50/pc at volume. Chenille patches.

Leather

Debossed, embossed or foil-pressed leather for a heritage look. Pilot $0.60/pc, then $0.04–$0.25/pc at volume. Leather patches.

Styles

Jacket patch styles from the floor

Placement decides size and weight: a chest patch and a back piece are different builds even when the artwork is the same.

Size guide from our varsity notes: jackets take about 6 in for a chest or sleeve patch, back lettering is normally 8 in and up, and mascots or icons order as 3–5 in patches.

FAQ

Jacket patch questions, answered

What is the minimum order for jacket patches?

50 pcs per design, whichever construction you choose.

Which construction is best for a jacket?

Chenille and leather read as heritage, embroidery carries fine detail. The choice follows the artwork rather than the price.

Can jacket patches be iron-on?

Heat seal is available, but sew-on is the default for jackets because it survives industrial washing and daily wear better than any adhesive route. Heat-test on the actual fabric before bulk.

What size should a jacket patch be?

Jackets take about 6 in for a chest or sleeve patch; back lettering is normally 8 in and up; mascots and icons order as 3–5 in patches.

How long does production take?

10–12 days after proof approval, and the digital proof is issued within 1 business day after artwork.
