Inside the factory

Inside SAIM Label: Weaving, Embroidery and Finishing in Dongguan

Founded in 2014, SAIM Label runs seven garment-trim processes in Dongguan. This page follows real workshop photographs through weaving, embroidery, cutting, folding, finishing, sample checks and finished-piece examples.

Core production

Weaving and embroidery use different production routes

The right route follows the artwork: woven construction for flat fine detail, embroidery for raised stitch texture.

Computer weaving line

Woven labels, woven patches and woven key tags begin with artwork translated into yarn structure.

Buyer inputs: finished size, artwork detail, yarn colors, fold or edge route.

Weaving-machine detail

The close view shows the yarn-fed weaving route used for flat, high-detail trim constructions.

Buyer inputs: minimum text size, weave density, face and reverse requirements.

Multi-head embroidery

Embroidery builds raised stitch texture for badge-style patches and embroidered keychains.

Buyer inputs: patch size, stitch coverage, backing, border and thread colors.

Cutting and finishing

Shape, edge and fold are production decisions

Not every product uses every machine. The approved construction determines whether the order needs laser cutting, ultrasonic slitting, automatic folding or manual edge finishing.

Laser cutting

Custom contours and irregular patch shapes are cut to the approved outline.

Ultrasonic slitting

Ultrasonic processing creates sealed cut edges on suitable woven goods.

Automatic folding

Approved fold type and finished dimensions guide the label-folding route.

Edge sewing and finishing

Manual finishing handles edge, attachment and final-detail operations that depend on the product.

The seven processes

From artwork to finished trim, step by step

01

Computer weaving

The core line. Artwork is translated to yarn and woven as damask, satin or taffeta labels, patches and keychain bodies.

02

Label printing

Care labels and printed satin or nylon labels with composition, size and wash data.

03

Screen printing

Flat color prints on labels and tapes where woven yarn is not the right route.

04

Embroidery

Raised stitch texture for badge-style patches and embroidered keychains.

05

Heat transfer

Tagless transfer labels applied directly to fabric for skin-contact positions.

06

Ultrasonic cutting

Clean sealed edges on woven goods without fraying.

07

Laser cutting

Custom patch shapes and fine contours cut to the artwork outline.

Before shipment

What the team checks

  • Artwork clarity and spelling against the approved proof
  • Color within the available yarn or print route
  • Cut size, fold position and edge feel
  • Backing adhesion, eyelet strength or hardware placement where applicable
  • Packing count per bundle, roll or carton

Approved designs, yarn choices and machine settings stay in the ERP so repeat orders can follow the recorded setup.
