Choose it when

Choose PVC when the patch must survive water, mud and abrasion, or when the brand wants a modern molded look with crisp raised edges. Color zones stay sharp and the patch keeps its shape outdoors.

Check another route when

Avoid PVC when the buyer expects a fabric feel or needs iron-on application: PVC cannot be heat-pressed onto garments. Plan sew-on channels or hook-and-loop from the start.

FAQ

PVC Patches questions, answered

Are PVC patches waterproof?

Yes. Molded PVC handles rain, mud and washing better than fabric patches, which is why outdoor and tactical programs use it.

Can PVC patches be ironed on?

No. PVC cannot be heat-pressed. The standard routes are a molded sew channel for machine sewing or hook-and-loop backing for removable use.

What is the difference between 2D and 3D PVC patches?

2D uses flat stacked color layers with sharp edges; 3D adds sculpted relief and rounded depth. 3D molds cost more but give a stronger emblem look.

Do repeat orders need a new mold?

No. The mold is kept for repeat production of the same design, which shortens lead time on reorders.
