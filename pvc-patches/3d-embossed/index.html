Tonal relief

Custom 3D Embossed PVC Patches

SAIM Label molds 3D embossed PVC patches in Dongguan: MOQ 50 pcs per design, from $0.60/pc, relief molded in the same colour as the base, production 10–12 days after proof. The design is carried by depth and shadow, not by colour.

What changes without colour

The design has to hold up in shadow

A colour version and an embossed version of the same logo are not the same artwork. Colour can carry a shape that has no depth; embossing cannot invent depth the drawing never had.

Contrast comes from light

There is one material and one colour, so the only thing separating two areas is the wall between them and the shadow that wall throws. Every boundary in the artwork has to become a physical step.

Everything is a decision

In a printed design the flat areas are simply where nothing was drawn. Here every region has to be told whether it sits on the base, rises above it, or sinks below it.

It reads at one angle, not all

Tonal relief depends on the light hitting it. Straight on, an embossed patch is quiet; in raking light the same patch resolves completely. Plan for that, not for a flat catalogue view.

Artwork that survives

What disappears in the mould

Tooling cannot form a wall thinner than the mould can cut, so any element narrower than that simply is not there when the patch comes out. In practice the casualties are always the same: hairlines in a serif, the thin strokes of a script logo, small counters inside letters, dots in a registered mark, and any detail that only worked because it was printed at high resolution.

The fix is not to shrink the design. It is to decide what the patch is actually saying and drop the rest — thicken the strokes that carry meaning, replace a cluster of small marks with one raised plane, and keep the negative space wide enough that the base reads as background rather than as a groove.

Send the artwork at finished size in vector. We look at the smallest element first, mark the ones that will not form, and come back with the specific edits before tooling. Retooling after a rejected sample costs more than that conversation.

Two directions, easy to confuse

Embossed, debossed, or both

Embossed

The element stands above the base surface. It catches light on its face and casts a shadow on one side, which is why lettering has to be thick enough to hold that face.

Debossed

The element is pressed below the base. It reads as a recess: shadow inside the cavity, light on the far wall. Useful for fine marks, because a cut line can be finer than a standing ridge.

Both can live on the same patch, and that combination is often what makes a tonal design legible: the main mark raised, the supporting linework cut in. Specify which is which on the artwork rather than leaving it to be inferred — reversing them changes the whole read.

Why buyers pick it

Tone-on-tone instead of a coloured badge

  • The mark is present but quiet. A tonal embossed patch sits on the garment as texture, not as a badge, which is what a premium or minimal brand usually wants.
  • It works in one colour. Black on black, sand on sand, a single house colour across a range. There is no colour matching to approve.
  • It gives a garment label volume. Where a woven label would be flat, an embossed PVC patch adds physical depth at the same footprint.
  • Nothing to fade. There is no print layer and no second material, so wear shows as surface texture rather than as rubbed-off colour.
Cost

What changes the price on a tonal patch

Molded PVC patches start at $0.60/pc at the 50 pcs minimum, with volume production at $0.19–$0.40/pc. On a tonal embossed patch the usual cost driver is absent: there are no colour-fill steps, so a ten-colour crest and a single-colour mark are not priced differently for colour. What moves the number instead is finished size, how many relief levels the design needs, the backing, and how much of the mould has to be cut as fine detail.

Relief levels are the line worth watching. One raised plane is simpler tooling than a design that steps through three heights, and the difference is real work on the mould rather than a packaging choice.

The mould is a one-time cost tied to the silhouette and is reused on repeat orders, so a reorder of an approved design does not start from tooling again.

Backing

How the patch is fixed

Sew channel

A groove moulded into the edge for machine stitching. Permanent, lowest profile, and the usual choice when the patch is a garment label rather than a badge.

Hook-and-loop

Removable and interchangeable, with a loop panel sewn to the garment once. The right route when the patch has to come off for washing or change with the program.

Adhesive

Peel-and-stick for hard goods and packs. A temporary fix on fabric, not a production closure.

Iron-on is not available at any of these. PVC softens under a heat press and takes the shape of whatever is pressing it, so a heat-set backing cannot be bonded to this material.

Procurement

What to send

  • Minimum — 50 pcs per design, backing included.
  • Lead time — bulk production 10–12 days after the proof is approved.
  • Artwork — AI, PDF, SVG, EPS, CDR, or a clean PNG/JPG at finished size, with the quantity and the placement.
  • Relief instruction — say which elements rise, which are recessed, and whether the patch is tonal or colour-filled. This is the instruction that most often goes missing.
  • Artwork rights — band, brand, club and unit marks must be supplied by you with the right to reproduce them. We do not copy third-party trademarks or official insignia on request.
Questions

3D embossed PVC patch questions

What does embossed mean on a PVC patch?

The design is raised in the same PVC as the base, with no second colour and no print. The shape is read from the moulded relief and the shadow it casts, not from colour contrast.

Will thin lines survive embossing?

Often not. A stroke has to be wide enough for the mould to form a standing wall, and hairlines, fine serifs and thin script usually disappear or break up. Send the final-size vector and we will mark the elements that need opening up before tooling starts.

What is the difference between embossed and debossed?

Embossed is raised above the base surface; debossed is pressed below it. Both can sit on the same patch. Decide which elements rise and which sink, because reversing them changes how the artwork reads in raking light.

Can an embossed patch also carry colour?

Yes, but it becomes a different product. Once colour zones are added the design reads through colour as well as depth, which is the standard molded PVC route. Tonal embossing is chosen when colour is deliberately absent.

What is the minimum order and lead time?

50 pcs per design, from $0.60/pc at that minimum, with bulk production running 10–12 days after the proof is approved. The proof fixes relief depth, silhouette and backing together.
