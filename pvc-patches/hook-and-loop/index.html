Custom Hook-and-Loop PVC Patches

SAIM Label molds hook-and-loop PVC patches in Dongguan: MOQ 50 pcs per design, from $0.60/pc, soft PVC die-cut to any silhouette, production 10–12 days after proof. The backing is the point — the patch lifts off the garment and goes back on.

What this backing buys you

One garment, several patches

Hook-and-loop is chosen when the patch is not permanent. The garment keeps a sewn loop panel; the patches become interchangeable parts.

Swap without resewing

A unit, a venue or a season changes, and the same jacket takes a different patch. Nothing is unpicked from the garment.

No stitching through the shell

On coated, waterproof or padded shells, a needle leaves holes that stay. Only the loop panel is sewn, and it can be placed once at the pattern stage.

Wash the garment, not the patch

The patch lifts off before laundering, which keeps the moulded face and the loop pile out of the drum.

The mistake to avoid

Which half goes on the patch

Hook-and-loop is two different materials. The hook side is the stiff, scratchy face; the loop side is the soft, fuzzy panel. The convention that holds up in use is: hook laminated to the patch back, loop sewn onto the garment.

Reversed, it still grips on the first press, which is why the error survives sampling and shows up later. A loop panel that rides on the removable piece gets crushed, matted and loaded with lint every time it is handled, and it is the loop face that decides how many cycles the closure survives. Put the loop where it will be protected and the hook on the part that gets replaced.

Say which way round you want it on the purchase order. If you have no preference, this is the arrangement we build.

Compare the three routes

Sew channel, hook-and-loop or adhesive

Sew channel

A groove moulded into the patch edge so a machine can stitch it down. Permanent, lowest profile, and the cheapest of the three because it adds no separate component.

Hook-and-loop

Removable and interchangeable. Adds a loop panel to the garment and a hook face to the patch, so it is the route with the most material in it — and the only one that lets the patch come off.

Adhesive

Peel-and-stick for hard goods, packs and short campaigns. Not a garment closure: adhesive on fabric is a temporary fix, not a production method.

None of the three can be replaced by iron-on. PVC softens under a heat press and takes the shape of whatever is pressing it, so a heat-set backing is not an option for this material at any temperature that would actually bond.

Cost

What a hook-and-loop PVC patch costs

Molded PVC patches start at $0.60/pc at the 50 pcs minimum, with volume production at $0.19–$0.40/pc. Four things move the number: finished size, the number of molded levels, the colour count, and the backing.

Size and colour count are the two that surprise buyers. Each solid colour zone is filled separately and gradients have to be split into layers, so a six-colour crest costs more to mould than a two-colour mark of the same size. The mould itself is a one-time cost tied to the silhouette — it is reused on repeat orders, which is why a reorder of an approved design does not start from the tooling again.

Backing is quoted as its own line because it is a separate operation. A sew channel is moulded into the patch; hook-and-loop is a component added and fixed to the back.

Where it gets used

Programs that specify removable patches

  • Uniform and duty gear — unit, division or role identifiers that change with assignment rather than with the jacket.
  • Outdoor and airsoft — team and event patches swapped between games, with the loop panel sewn to the carrier once.
  • Corporate and event merch — one base cap or bag, a different patch per campaign or per year.
  • Collector and morale patches — the format where a molded relief and a clean die-cut edge carry the whole design.

Buyers usually search for this closure under the name Velcro, which is a brand; the generic term is hook-and-loop, and it is the term we quote against.

Questions

Hook-and-loop PVC patch questions

Which half of the hook-and-loop goes on the patch?

The stiff hook side is laminated to the patch back and the soft loop side is sewn onto the garment. Reversing them still grips once, but the loop panel wears out far faster when it sits on the removable piece.

Can a PVC patch be ironed on instead?

No. PVC softens and deforms under a heat press, so iron-on is not an option for this material. Choose a sew channel, hook-and-loop or adhesive from the start.

What is the minimum order for hook-and-loop PVC patches?

50 pcs per design. The mould is made once and reused on repeat orders, so a later reorder of the same design does not repeat the tooling step.

Will the patch stay on during washing?

The loop panel is sewn to the garment, so the garment side survives normal laundering. Remove the patch before washing when the garment allows it — repeated tumbling is what degrades the loop face.

Can hook-and-loop be added to a design I already approved?

If the mould already exists, the backing is decided at order and does not change the moulded front. Tell us before the proof so the backing is fixed in the same approval.
