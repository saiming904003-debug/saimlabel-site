What a woven neck label is

The neck label is the first thing a buyer reads on a garment — and the label that carries your brand name from the rack to the repeat order. Woven neck labels use fine yarns to build the text and logo directly into the fabric, so the brand mark stays sharp after years of washing. They are the standard choice when the logo needs clean text, durable color and a soft, comfortable finish against the skin.

Folds

Folds decide how the label is sewn in and how it sits:

  • End fold (Manhattan fold) — the label folds at both ends before sewing, hiding the raw edges; the classic neck label look.
  • Center fold — folded in half over the seam edge, creating a symmetrical flag.
  • Flat cut — no fold, clean cut edges, a modern minimal look for flat-sewn labels.

The full fold options and their sewing routes are in the woven label fold guide.

Neck label questions, answered

What size are woven neck labels?

Most brand neck labels run about 50 x 20 mm finished (2 x 0.8 in) — and any size is cut to spec. The finished (visible) size is what matters; folds add their own allowance.

What is the difference between a neck label and a care label?

The neck label carries the brand name and logo and sits inside the back collar. The care label carries composition, washing symbols and origin, and sits at the side seam. Most garments use both.

Which folds are available?

End fold (Manhattan fold) for the classic look, center fold for a symmetrical flag, and flat cut for a clean modern edge. The fold changes the sewing route, so it is decided before production.

What is the MOQ for woven neck labels?

MOQ starts from 50 pieces per design for main products. Pricing is quoted by size, construction and quantity after the artwork is confirmed.
