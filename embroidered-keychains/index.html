Choose it when

Choose embroidery keychains when the buyer wants a thicker tactile tag. They are useful when the merchandise set already includes embroidered patches or uniform badges.

Check another route when

Avoid overly detailed artwork. Embroidered keychains need readable shapes and enough tag width for thread coverage.

Honest buyer notes

Read before you finalize artwork

  • Embroidery thickness limits small text and fine line art.
  • Double-sided embroidery makes the tag thicker and should be planned early.
  • Hardware placement needs enough blank margin for reinforcement.
FAQ

Embroidery Keychains questions, answered

What is the difference between woven and embroidered keychains?

Woven keychains are flatter and better for fine detail. Embroidered keychains are thicker and more textured.

Can the front and back be different?

Yes. Front and back designs can differ, depending on artwork complexity and construction.

Can embroidery keychains match patch orders?

Yes. Embroidered patches and keychains can be produced as a coordinated merchandise set.

Are embroidered keychains suitable for small text?

They can hold readable text, but very small lettering should usually be simplified or moved to a woven or printed element.
