Satin labelSmooth shiny surface for lingerie, scarves, premium neck labels and soft-touch branding.
Taffeta labelEconomical woven option for bulk size labels, care-size combinations and simple brand trims.

Folds & finishing

How labels fold, cut and sew

Center foldLoop-style label for side seams, hems and small brand tabs.
End foldClean folded side edges for neck labels that must be sewn on both ends.
Miter foldAngled folded ends for woven labels used on sleeves, hems or outer branding.
Straight cutFlat cut labels for packaging, outerwear and applications where edges are fully sewn down.
Patch construction

Borders, backings and edges for patches

Merrow borderClassic raised edge for round, rectangle, oval and shield patches.
Laser cut edgeClean custom shapes for modern labels, badges and detailed silhouettes.
Heat cut edgeEconomical flat edge for bulk woven patches and simple forms.
Satin stitch borderA tidy stitched outline when the design needs a controlled edge.
Sew-on backingMost secure route for garments, hats, bags and long-term use.
Iron-on backingHeat seal backing for heat press application after buyer fabric testing.
Hook-and-loopRemovable backing for uniforms, outdoor gear and club patches.
Adhesive backingShort-term placement or packaging use; not a replacement for sewing on garments.
