Reading the code

What 9144190032491022XY means

A Chinese Unified Social Credit Code is 18 characters and every part is checkable. Ours breaks down as follows — you can confirm the Dongguan part from the code alone.

Check us yourself

Three steps, two minutes

1 · Open the official platform

Go to the National Unified Social Credit Code public query platform at cods.org.cn/gscx, run by China's National Organisation Code Data Service Centre. It is a government platform — there is nothing of ours to install or agree to.

2 · Search the name or the code

Enter the Chinese name 东莞市赛铭电脑织造有限公司, or paste the code 9144190032491022XY. Either one is enough.

3 · Compare three fields

The record should match the registered name, the credit code and the Dongcheng address listed above. The same details also appear in China's National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System.

Before you pay

Verify the beneficiary, not just the website

Company-identity fraud usually happens after the quote, not before it. Two habits close that gap:

  • Ask for the beneficiary name on our proforma invoice, and compare it with the registered name on this page before you send any payment.
  • If it does not match, stop. Contact us through this website or WhatsApp on the number in the footer, and confirm before transferring funds.
  • Bank changes are never announced by email alone. Treat any message that changes payment details as suspicious until you have confirmed it on a channel you already used.

We would rather answer a verification question twice than have a buyer pay the wrong account.

One entity, two names

Why the Chinese and English names differ

Buyers comparing our website with a licence or a platform profile often find two different names. Both are correct.

  • The Chinese name is the legal one. 东莞市赛铭电脑织造有限公司 is what appears on the business licence, on contracts and in government and platform records.
  • The Chinese name describes the method. The wording covers computerised weaving — the core process the factory was registered around, and still the process behind our woven labels, patches and keychains.
  • The English name covers the range. "Dongguan Saiming Garment Accessories Co., Ltd." is the trading name we use on this website and in English-language correspondence, because trim buyers know the products by category, not by process.
  • SAIM Label is the brand. It is our short public name, used on sample cards, packaging and social profiles.
  • They are one legal entity, not a group. There is no parent company, no trading company in between, and no second factory operating under a similar name.

Verification FAQ

Questions buyers ask before trusting a supplier

What is the registered company name behind SAIM Label?

The registered Chinese legal name is 东莞市赛铭电脑织造有限公司, with Unified Social Credit Code 9144190032491022XY, registered in Dongcheng Subdistrict, Dongguan, Guangdong. SAIM Label and "Dongguan Saiming Garment Accessories Co., Ltd." are the English trading names.

Why is the English name not a translation of the Chinese name?

The registered Chinese name describes the core manufacturing method, while the English trading name covers the wider product range we sell. One entity, two names — we do not use a separate English-only legal entity.

How can I verify the company myself?

Search the company name or the credit code 9144190032491022XY on China's National Unified Social Credit Code public query platform at cods.org.cn/gscx. The result should match the name, code and address on this page. No login and no contact with us is required.

Can I get a copy of the business licence?

Yes, on request. We send it directly to the buyer rather than publishing the scan, because the licence shows the legal representative's name. Ask through the quote form and we will send it with the quotation.

How do I avoid payment fraud?
