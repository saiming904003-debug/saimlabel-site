Three constructions

Choose the construction before the motif

Holiday patches are built one of three ways here. Each has its own published price band, and all three run in-house, so a mixed holiday order is not split between suppliers. For the trade-offs between them, see woven vs embroidered patches.

Embroidered on felt

Thread stitched onto a felt base with a merrow edge. The usual route for outlines, lettering and character artwork.

Pilot $0.65/pc · $0.19–$0.38/pc at volume · 95 catalog designs

Chenille

Raised towel-loop yarn on a felt base with merrow borders — the varsity look, best on single letters, numbers and simple shapes.

Pilot $0.65/pc · $0.25–$0.50/pc at volume · 42 catalog designs

Felt die-cut

Solid felt cut to the outline with no stitch fill. The lowest-cost route, used for small ornaments, tags and kidswear.

Pilot $0.60/pc · $0.15–$0.39/pc at volume · 54 catalog designs

FAQ

Christmas patch questions, answered

What is the minimum order for Christmas patches?

50 pcs per design. A mixed holiday assortment is fine as long as each design reaches the 50 pcs minimum.

Which construction should I choose for holiday designs?

Embroidery on felt for outlines, lettering and character artwork; chenille when you want a raised varsity texture; felt die-cut when the shape alone carries the design and cost matters.

How long does a Christmas patch order take?

Production runs 10–12 days after you approve the proof. The proof is issued before production starts, so artwork is settled first.

Can the patches be iron-on for retail packs?

Yes. Heat seal backing is available alongside sew-on, adhesive and hook-and-loop. Test heat settings on the final garment fabric before a full run.

Do you make custom shapes and sizes?

Yes. Patches are die-cut to your artwork, so the outline follows the design rather than a fixed die.
