Who we make it for

Patches, Jet Tags and Trims for Streetwear

Streetwear brands use SAIM Label for flat woven logo patches that hold thin lines, chenille back pieces with retro texture, double-sided woven jet tags for drops, and kraft hang tags — the whole drop kit from one floor, at MOQs that fit limited runs (many catalog items from 50 pieces).

Custom Woven PatchesFlat, detailed logo patches for denim, jackets and caps — thin lines and small type stay sharp.

Custom Hang TagsKraft or heavyweight tags that carry the drop story at retail.
Buyer notes

What experienced buyers specify up front

  • Limited drops do not need bulk MOQs — check the per-item MOQs in the gallery and price two or three quantity tiers in one RFQ.
  • Jet tags are double-sided by construction: plan front and back artwork together instead of leaving the reverse blank.
  • Gradient-heavy artwork gets split into woven-friendly color blocks — approve the split on the proof, not after production.
  • Mixing patches, tags and labels in one order keeps colors consistent and ships as one consolidated parcel.

RFQ checklist

  • Artwork per item (front and back for jet tags)
  • Product mix for the drop: patches / tags / labels / keychains
  • Quantity per item and drop date
  • Hardware color for keychains (silver, black, antique, gold tone)
  • Packing preference: bulk or per-set

FAQ

Streetwear Brands questions

Do you support small runs for limited drops?

Yes. Many catalog items start from 50 pieces; the gallery shows the real MOQ per sample so you can plan the drop before asking.

Can jet tags have different front and back designs?

Yes. Double-sided woven construction carries different front and back layouts — logo one side, drop name or date on the other.

Can one order combine patches, jet tags and hang tags?

Yes. Everything runs in one factory, so a drop kit is produced and shipped as one coordinated order.
