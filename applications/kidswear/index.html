Buyer notes

What experienced buyers specify up front

  • For kidswear, edge feel and fold choice matter more than a small unit-price difference — specify soft hand feel in the RFQ.
  • Small decorative parts (sequins, beads) on babywear must pass the buyer's market attachment rules; review this before artwork approval.
  • Tagless transfers should be wash-tested on the actual garment fabric — settings are supplied with each order.
  • US and EU care label requirements differ; our compliance guide lists what each market expects before production.

FAQ

Kidswear & Babywear questions

What is the softest labeling option for babywear?

Tagless heat-transfer prints have no sewn edge at all. Among sewn labels, printed satin and fine damask with reviewed folds are the usual soft routes.

Are felt patches safe for kidswear?

Felt itself is soft; the review point is attachment strength and any small decorative parts, which should be checked against the buyer's market requirements before bulk.

Can you write the care instructions for us?

We format and produce the label, but fiber content and care wording must be confirmed by the brand or importer for each target market — our US/EU guide shows what to prepare.
