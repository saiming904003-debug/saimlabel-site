Who we make it for

Patches and Labels for Hats and Caps

Hat and cap suppliers order woven front patches when lettering must stay clean at typical 6–8 cm widths, 3D puff embroidery when the logo should pop on the crown, and leather or PVC fronts for premium or outdoor lines. SAIM Label runs all four constructions, so one RFQ can compare them on look and price.

Custom Woven PatchesThe route for fine lettering and detailed crests on front panels — thin lines survive at cap sizes.

Buyer notes

What experienced buyers specify up front

  • Regular shapes (rounds, shields, rectangles) take a merrow border cleanly; irregular silhouettes should go laser-cut instead.
  • Structured cap fronts are curved — keep critical text away from the patch edge and confirm the size on a flat proof plus a mounted photo sample.
  • Sew-on is the reliable route on caps; iron-on backing on structured panels should be heat-tested before bulk.
  • Text that must read at arm's length needs bold weight — hairline typography belongs on the woven route, not embroidery.

FAQ

Hat & Cap Suppliers questions

Which patch type is best for cap fronts?

Woven patches for fine detail and small text; embroidered or 3D puff for bold raised logos; leather for premium lines; PVC for outdoor durability. The artwork decides — send it and we suggest.

Can you match the patch to our cap fabric color?

Send a fabric reference or Pantone code with the artwork; color is confirmed on the proof against yarn and material limits.

What is the minimum order for hat patches?

MOQ depends on construction; many catalog patch items start from 50 pieces — see the gallery MOQs or ask with your artwork.
