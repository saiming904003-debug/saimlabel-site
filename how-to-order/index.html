Ordering

How to Order Custom Trims from SAIM Label

Five steps from artwork to shipped cartons. No account, no portal — artwork plus a clear brief is enough to start.

Send artwork & requirements

Logo or artwork file (AI, PDF, SVG, EPS or clear PNG/JPG), product type, size, quantity, application and target date.

Get a quote

We reply with material and construction suggestions, unit price by quantity tier, and sampling arrangement.

Approve the digital proof

A production proof confirms size, colors, fold or border, backing and layout before anything is made.

Pre-production sample

A physical sample for approval; repeats of the same design skip ahead and run faster.

Bulk production & QC

Weaving, printing or embroidery in-house, then per-batch checks: logo clarity, color, size, edge, backing and packing count before shipment.

Prepare this

What a complete RFQ includes

  • Artwork file — vector (AI, PDF, SVG, CDR, EPS) preferred; clear PNG/JPG works for review
  • Product type and application (garment, hat, bag, uniform, merch…)
  • Finished size and quantity — tiers welcome, e.g. 500 / 1,000 / 5,000
  • Construction preference if known: fold, border, backing, hardware
  • Target market and delivery date
Before shipment

What we check every batch

  • Logo clarity and spelling against the approved proof
  • Color check against yarn or print limits, not only screens
  • Cut size, fold position and edge feel
  • Backing adhesion, eyelet strength and hardware placement
  • Packing count per bundle, roll or carton before shipment
FAQ

Ordering questions

How do I get a quote from SAIM Label?

Send your artwork, product type, size, quantity and application by WhatsApp, email or the quote form. We reply with material suggestions and tiered pricing.

What artwork files do you accept?

Vector files (AI, PDF, SVG, CDR, EPS) are best. High-resolution PNG or JPG can work for review and redraw.

Do repeat orders get faster?

Yes. Approved designs, molds and production settings are kept on file, so repeat production skips sampling and runs faster.

What is your minimum order quantity?

MOQ depends on the product and construction. Many catalog items start from 50 pieces, and some from even less — check each product page or ask with your artwork.
