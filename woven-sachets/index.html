Choose it when

Choose woven sachets when the brand item should be kept, not consumed — gift boxes, seasonal gifting, hotel amenities, wedding favors and retail add-ons. The artwork is woven into the shell, so it carries color and detail that outlasts print.

Check another route when

Compliance content belongs on a woven or care label inside the garment, not on a sachet. And very small lettering reads better on the woven side tag than on a shaped, filled edge.

Honest buyer notes

Read before you finalize sachet artwork

  • Simple outlines read best on small filled shapes; intricate edges blur as size drops.
  • Brand lettering fits better on the woven side tag than along a shaped, filled edge.
  • Cord color, knot style and side-tag text are confirmed per order before production.
FAQ

Woven Sachets questions, answered

Can woven sachets be scented?

Yes. Scented fill can be specified alongside standard cotton stuffing, so the sachet carries fragrance as well as brand artwork.

What is the MOQ and pilot price for woven sachets?

MOQ is 50 pcs per design, with pilot runs from $1.00/pc. The final quote moves with size, shape, fill and quantity.

Can you match an existing shape or sample?

Yes. Shapes follow your artwork — send reference photos or drawings with sizes and we confirm feasibility before quoting.

Do sachets replace woven labels in a program?

They complement them. Labels carry compliance and care content inside the garment; sachets carry brand presence in packaging, gifting and retail.
