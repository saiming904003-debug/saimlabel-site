Custom Sew-On Woven Patches

What sew-on buyers specify

Shape, border and where it sits

Sew-on is decided by the garment, not by preference. The surface underneath and the way the patch is fixed both have to be settled before artwork.

The surface

A heat press needs a flat area and a fabric that tolerates its temperature. Seams, hardware, coatings and leather fail one of those tests, so the patch is stitched instead.

The border

A laser-cut edge follows any outline and stays flat. A merrow border wraps the raw edge in thread, which suits round and shield shapes and heavier stitch detail.

The placement

Back, chest, sleeve, pocket or bag panel. Placement decides the finished size, and the finished size is what the price is built on.

Why this backing

When sew-on is the right answer

Heat transfer needs two things a finished garment often cannot give: a flat surface, and a fabric that tolerates the press temperature. Bag panels with seams and hardware, coated fabrics, leather, and garments that are already assembled all fail one of those tests. A sewn patch sidesteps the question — the border is stitched through the material, so how well it holds depends on thread, not on the surface underneath.

The cost of that reliability is machine time. Sewing is a separate operation, so sew-on is specified when the patch has to outlast the garment, or when the substrate rules heat out entirely.

Cost

What a sew-on woven patch costs

Woven patches start at $0.60/pc at the 50 pcs minimum. Four things move the number: finished size, thread density, the colour count, and the border. A merrow border is a separate sewing operation from a laser-cut edge, and that difference shows on the quote — the cut shape does not add a sewing step, the wrapped border does.

Artwork complexity rarely changes the price on its own. A dense music or band logo runs on the same weaving setup as a simple mark; what it changes is how much detail survives, which is the reason woven gets chosen over embroidery for fine linework.

Choose the backing

Sew-on or iron-on, same patch

Sew-on when

Bag panels, leather and coated fabric, heavy jacket backs, workwear, or a garment that is already assembled. Also the safe choice where industrial laundering has to be survived.

Iron-on when

Flat cotton or poly-cotton blanks, patches applied before the garment is sewn, or when the application has to be fast and repeatable in-house.

The patch itself is identical either way — the backing is decided at order, on the same approved artwork.

Shape and edge

Irregular shapes are cut, not woven

Any outline in your artwork becomes the patch outline. Laser cutting holds tight curves and small internal cut-outs; a merrow border wraps the edge in thread for a traditional finish. Send the shape as a vector path — a JPG outline has to be traced, which introduces a decision you did not make.

Procurement

What to send

  • Minimum — 50 pcs per design, sew-on included.
  • Lead time — bulk production 10–12 days after the proof is approved.
  • Artwork — AI, PDF, SVG, EPS, CDR, or a clean PNG/JPG, with the finished size and the quantity.
  • Artwork rights — band, brand and club logos must be supplied by you with the right to reproduce them. We do not copy third-party trademarks on request.
Questions

Sew-on woven patch questions

Can a sew-on patch go on a bag?

Yes. Bag panels, pockets and straps are one of the commonest sew-on uses, because seams and hardware make a flat heat press impractical.

Can the same design be made with iron-on backing instead?

Yes. The woven patch is the same; only the backing changes. Tell us which one at order rather than after the proof.

What shapes can a sew-on woven patch be?

Any outline that can be cut — round, shield, rectangular or fully irregular with internal cut-outs. Vector artwork keeps the shape exact.

What is the minimum order for sew-on patches?

50 pcs per design. Sampling runs use the same artwork and the same approved proof as bulk.

How long does a sew-on patch order take?

Bulk production runs 10–12 days after the proof is approved. The proof fixes size, colours, border and backing before the line starts.
