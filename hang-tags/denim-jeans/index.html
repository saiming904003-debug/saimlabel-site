What denim buyers specify

Board, attachment and data panels

A jeans tag carries more than a logo: retail price, size and barcode often share the same board. Those three decisions are settled before artwork, not after.

Board and finish

Kraft, coated art card or textured premium board, with recycled stock on request. Board weight decides how premium the tag feels in hand, so approve a physical sample before bulk.

Attachment

String with eyelet, safety pin or plastic barb. The attachment is confirmed at the quote, and the punch position is planned together with the layout.

Data panels

Size, price, barcode, QR and a care summary can all be planned into one layout, so the tag does not need a second printing pass later.

Styles

Denim tag styles we print

Full colour on both sides, plus foil, emboss and spot finishes. Send the artwork and the panel layout you need.

Very thin foil lines can break up during printing — keep foil artwork at printable line weights.

FAQ

Denim hang tag questions, answered

What is the minimum order for denim hang tags?

50 pcs per design. Mixed sizes or finishes are quoted as separate items.

How much does a jeans hang tag cost?

Pilot runs at MOQ start at $0.20/pc typical; volume production runs $0.04–$0.26/pc depending on board, size, attachment and quantity.

How long does production take?

10–12 days after proof approval. The digital proof is issued within 1 business day after artwork.

Can the tag carry price, size and barcode?

Yes — size, price, barcode, QR and a care summary panel can all be planned into the layout before printing.

Can I get a physical sample first?

Yes. Approve a physical sample before bulk, because board weight and finish decide how the tag feels in hand.
