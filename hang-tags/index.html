Choose it when

Choose custom hang tags when the retail presentation matters: they carry brand story, price, size and barcode data, and they complete the trim set alongside woven labels and care labels from the same factory.

Check another route when

Avoid choosing a paper stock by photo alone. Perceived quality comes from thickness and surface texture, so confirm the board weight and finish on a physical sample before bulk printing.

Honest buyer notes

Read before you finalize artwork

  • Board weight decides how premium the tag feels in hand; approve a physical sample before bulk.
  • Very thin foil lines can break up; keep foil artwork at printable line weights.
  • String and pin attachment is manual work; confirm whether tags ship attached or separate.
FAQ

Hang Tags questions, answered

What paper is best for hang tags?

Kraft suits natural and denim looks, coated art card suits full-color retail programs, and heavyweight textured boards suit premium lines. The right answer follows the brand position and budget.

Can hang tags include barcodes and prices?

Yes. Barcode, price, size and QR panels can be printed directly or added as a smaller second tag; send the final data with the artwork.

Do hang tags come with strings attached?

Both routes are possible: tags with strings and eyelets pre-assembled, or tags and strings packed separately for the garment factory to attach.

Can hang tags match my woven labels?

Yes. Producing tags, woven labels and care labels together keeps color and brand style consistent across the whole trim set.
