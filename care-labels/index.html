Read before you finalize artwork

  • Small text must remain readable after cutting and washing.
  • QR and barcode labels need scan testing at the final printed size.
  • Regulatory content should be confirmed by the buyer before production.
FAQ

Care Labels questions, answered

Can SAIM Label decide the care instruction for my garment?

We can format and produce the label, but the buyer or garment factory should confirm fiber content, care instructions, country of origin and target-market requirements.

Can care labels include multiple languages?

Yes. Multi-language care labels can be printed when the artwork and final text are provided.

Can you print QR codes or barcodes?

Yes. QR codes, batch codes and barcodes can be included if the buyer provides final data and size requirements.

What material is best for soft care labels?

Printed satin is often selected for soft apparel care labels. Cotton tape can fit natural-brand products when the look matters.
