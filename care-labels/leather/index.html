What changes

Three decisions before artwork

A leather garment is not washed like a cotton tee, so the label is specified around the cleaning method the brand declares rather than around the lowest price.

Symbols come from you

The care method and the symbols are set by the brand, importer or compliance advisor for the target market. We print the final text you approve; we do not choose the care standard.

Stock for the cleaning method

Satin polyester, nylon taffeta, cotton tape or heat transfer. The stock follows how the garment is actually cleaned, not the cheapest option in the list.

Print that stays readable

Thermal transfer, screen or digital printing depending on volume and design. Checks cover text clarity, scan test, wash resistance and cut accuracy.

Styles

Label styles we print

Fold and cut are chosen per garment: roll, straight cut, center fold, end fold or multi-panel.

Small text must remain readable after cutting and washing — that single constraint sets the minimum font size on the layout.

FAQ

Leather care label questions, answered

What is the minimum order for leather care labels?

50 pcs per design, on any stock we print.

Can you supply the care symbols for leather?

We print the final text and symbols you approve. The care standard is set by the brand or importer for the target market, not by the printer.

Which stock suits a leather garment?

Satin polyester, nylon taffeta, cotton tape or heat transfer — matched to how the garment is cleaned. Send the cleaning method and we suggest the stock.

Can labels be printed in several languages?

Yes. Multi-language care labels can be printed when the artwork and the final text are provided.

How long does production take?

10–12 days after proof approval, with the digital proof issued within 1 business day after artwork.
