Choose it when

Choose felt patches when the design is a colorful shape rather than a detailed logo: animals, hearts, stars, letters and seasonal motifs for kidswear, fashion basics and DIY kits.

Check another route when

Avoid felt for precise brand logos with small text. Felt edges and embroidery on felt are softer and rounder than woven detail, so fine artwork loses sharpness.

Honest buyer notes

Read before you finalize artwork

  • Sequin and small decorative parts on kidswear should be reviewed for attachment strength by the buyer's market rules.
  • Iron-on felt should be heat-tested: felt is thicker than woven patches and needs correct press settings.
  • Felt colors are batch-based; send color references early when exact matching matters.
FAQ

Heat Transfer Printing Patch questions, answered

Are felt patches suitable for kidswear?

Yes, felt is soft and colorful, which is why kidswear buyers use it. Attachment strength and any small decorative parts should be reviewed against the buyer's market requirements.

Can felt patches include embroidery?

Yes. Most felt patches combine a die-cut felt base with embroidered outlines, faces or text areas.

Can you make sequin patches?

Yes. Sequin zones or fully sequined shapes can be produced; the quote should state the sequin color and coverage.

What backing works best for craft kits?

Iron-on backing is the usual choice for DIY sets, with application instructions provided by the kit brand.
