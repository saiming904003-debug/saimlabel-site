When a buyer asks "is SAIM certified?", the accurate answer is that SAIM is a weaving and finishing factory whose yarn supply holds these certifications. For recycled-content or skin-contact claims on your labels, we confirm the chain on a per-order basis. See our honest guide: Recycled yarn and certifications, decoded.

Buyer due diligence

How to verify before you rely on a certificate

  • Holder — confirm who the certificate is issued to; a yarn mill's certificate does not automatically extend to the label factory downstream.
  • Scope — the certificate must cover the actual yarn and processing step you are buying.
  • Validity — certificates expire; ask for the current PDF and check the dates against the issuing body's database.
  • Claim fit — OEKO-TEX supports a harmful-substance story; only GRS/RCS paperwork supports a recycled-content story. Do not mix the two.
