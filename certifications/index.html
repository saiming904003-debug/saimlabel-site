Certifications and compliance

SAIM Label Certifications: OEKO-TEX® and GRS

SAIM Label operates with two third-party certifications — OEKO-TEX® STANDARD 100 and the Global Recycled Standard (GRS). This page states what each certificate covers, what it does not, and how a buyer can verify our certificates before placing an order.

What SAIM Label holds

Two certificates, two different claims

OEKO-TEX and GRS answer different buyer questions. One tests the product for harmful substances; the other traces recycled content through the supply chain. SAIM Label holds both — and we do not claim certificates we do not hold.

Harmful-substance testing

OEKO-TEX® STANDARD 100

STANDARD 100 by OEKO-TEX® is an independent testing and certification system for textile products. It tests against a defined list of harmful substances, covering the raw material, intermediate and end products at every processing stage.

  • Relevant to labels, trims and accessories that sit against the skin
  • Test criteria are stricter than many single-country legal limits
  • Renewed within the standard's validity cycle, as required by the scheme
Verify at oeko-tex.com →
Certificate scope and number are shared with buyers on request, together with the current PDF.
Recycled-content traceability

GRS — Global Recycled Standard

The Global Recycled Standard is a full-chain voluntary standard that verifies recycled content, enforces chain of custody and adds social and environmental requirements at each certified step.

  • Enables recycled-content claims on yarn used for woven labels and trims
  • Audited chain of custody, not a self-declared claim
  • Pairs with OEKO-TEX for buyers who need both safety and recycled proof
Standard owner: textileexchange.org →

Certificate numbers are intentionally not printed on this public page — send them to buyers together with the PDF so each buyer can check validity against the issuing body's database at the time of their order. See our honest guide: Recycled yarn and certifications, decoded.

Buyer due diligence

How to verify before you rely on a certificate

  • Scope — the certificate must cover the actual material and processing step you are buying, not a different product line.
  • Holder — confirm the certificate is issued to Dongguan Saiming Garment Accessories Co., Ltd., and check what the scope states.
  • Validity — certificates expire; ask for the current PDF and check the dates against the issuing body's database.
  • Claim fit — OEKO-TEX supports a harmful-substance story; only GRS/RCS paperwork supports a recycled-content story. Do not mix the two.

Ordering with certificates

Send artwork plus your compliance requirements — recycled yarn, skin-contact safety data, or both — and we confirm what the current certificates cover before quoting.
