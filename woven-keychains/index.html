Choose it when

Choose woven keychains when the artwork needs more detail and a lighter, flatter body than embroidery. They work well as add-on merchandise for apparel, car clubs and events.

Check another route when

Avoid very tiny details near the eyelet area. Keep the key tag readable at arm's length and reserve enough margin for reinforced stitching or hardware.

FAQ

Woven Keychains questions, answered

Can woven keychains be double sided?

Yes. Both sides can use the same artwork or different front and back designs, depending on the layout.

What size is common for woven jet tags?

A common jet tag size is around 13 x 3 cm, but the final size can be customized to the artwork and hardware.

Are woven keychains good for promotional gifts?

Yes. They are lightweight, durable and suitable for event gifts, club merchandise and brand add-on products.

What is the difference between woven and embroidered keychains?

Woven keychains are flatter and better for detail. Embroidered keychains are thicker and more tactile.
