Buyer FAQ

Custom Label, Patch, Keychain and Care Label FAQ

Short procurement answers for buyers comparing woven, embroidered and printed trim options.

Ordering & factory

How do I get a quote from SAIM Label?

Send your artwork, product type, size, quantity and application by WhatsApp, email or the quote form. We reply with material suggestions and tiered pricing.

Can I order woven labels, patches and keychains together?

Yes. Weaving, printing, embroidery and cutting run under one roof, so one artwork pack can become labels, patches, keychains and care labels in a single coordinated order.

What artwork files do you accept?

Vector files (AI, PDF, SVG, CDR, EPS) are best. High-resolution PNG or JPG can work for review and redraw.

Do you ship worldwide?

Yes. SAIM Label is export-focused; shipping method and terms are confirmed with the quote.

Do repeat orders get faster?

Yes. Approved designs, molds and production settings are kept on file, so repeat production skips sampling and runs faster.

What is your minimum order quantity?

MOQ depends on the product and construction. Many catalog items start from 50 pieces, and some from even less — check each product page or ask with your artwork.

Product questions

What woven label type is best for small text?

Damask woven labels are usually best because finer yarn can hold small text and detailed brand marks better than lower-density weaves.

Can SAIM Label make soft woven labels for kidswear?

Yes. For kidswear and activewear, the quote should specify soft hand feel, label position, fold type and edge requirements.

Are woven patches better than embroidered patches for small text?

Usually yes. Woven patches can show finer details because the artwork is woven into the fabric rather than stitched on top.

Can woven patches be ironed on?

Yes. Heat seal backing is available. Final heat press temperature, time and pressure should be tested on the buyer's garment fabric.

Can woven keychains be double sided?

Yes. Both sides can use the same artwork or different front and back designs, depending on the layout.

What size is common for woven jet tags?

A common jet tag size is around 13 x 3 cm, but the final size can be customized to the artwork and hardware.

Can SAIM Label decide the care instruction for my garment?

We can format and produce the label, but the buyer or garment factory should confirm fiber content, care instructions, country of origin and target-market requirements.

Can care labels include multiple languages?

Yes. Multi-language care labels can be printed when the artwork and final text are provided.

When should I choose embroidery instead of woven patches?

Choose embroidery when you want raised texture and a classic badge feel. Choose woven patches when the design has small text or fine detail.

Can you make Velcro embroidered patches?

Yes. Hook-and-loop backing is available for uniforms, tactical patches and removable name or team badges.

What is the difference between woven and embroidered keychains?

Woven keychains are flatter and better for fine detail. Embroidered keychains are thicker and more textured.

Can the front and back be different?

Yes. Front and back designs can differ, depending on artwork complexity and construction.

What are chenille patches best for?

Bold designs: varsity letters, numbers, mascots and simple logos on jackets, hoodies and caps. The raised loop texture is the main reason buyers choose chenille.

Are felt patches suitable for kidswear?

Yes, felt is soft and colorful, which is why kidswear buyers use it. Attachment strength and any small decorative parts should be reviewed against the buyer's market requirements.

Are PVC patches waterproof?

Yes. Molded PVC handles rain, mud and washing better than fabric patches, which is why outdoor and tactical programs use it.

Genuine leather or PU - which should I choose?

Genuine leather gives natural grain and premium aging with slight batch variation. PU gives consistent color, easier washing and lower cost, and suits vegan-positioned brands.

What paper is best for hang tags?

Kraft suits natural and denim looks, coated art card suits full-color retail programs, and heavyweight textured boards suit premium lines. The right answer follows the brand position and budget.

What are tagless labels?

A printed transfer applied to the garment by heat press instead of a sewn label: no edge, no itch, and no label for the customer to cut out.
