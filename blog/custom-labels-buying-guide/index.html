Custom Clothing Labels Buying Guide: Types, Costs, Choices

By the SAIM Label factory team · Updated 2026-07-02 · 7 min read

Short answer: Damask woven labels are the default brand label: fine detail, soft, durable. Satin is the soft-shine premium route, taffeta the economical size-run choice, printed satin carries care data, heat transfer goes tagless, and leather marks denim and heritage lines. Price follows construction, size and quantity — and the trim set is cheapest to coordinate when ordered together.

A short decision path

  • Is it the brand mark inside a garment? Damask woven; satin if the brand is soft-touch premium.
  • Does it carry data (size, care, origin)? Printed satin or nylon care labels; taffeta for woven size runs.
  • Does it touch sensitive skin? Tagless heat transfer, or damask/satin with reviewed folds and edges.
  • Is it exterior branding? That is patch territory — woven or embroidered — or a leather patch on denim and beanies.

How label cost actually behaves

Three levers move the price: construction (see the table), finished size, and quantity — unit prices step down at tier quantities, which is why RFQs should ask for two or three tiers. Artwork complexity barely moves woven label cost, unlike embroidery. And repeat orders are structurally cheaper in time: approved designs stay on file at SAIM Label, so reorders skip sampling and run faster.

Think in trim sets, not single items

A garment rarely ships with one label: brand label + size + care label + hang tag is the normal set. Ordering the set from one factory keeps color and tone consistent, consolidates shipping, and means one proof cycle instead of three vendors' worth. FAQ

What is the most economical woven label?

Taffeta. It is the standard route for bulk size labels and simple brand trims where budget beats hand feel.

Which label type is softest?

Tagless heat transfer removes the label entirely. Among sewn labels, printed satin and fine damask with reviewed folds are the soft routes.

Can one label combine brand, size and care?

Yes — book folds and multi-panel layouts carry brand on the face with size and care data inside; care content still needs brand confirmation per market.

How do I get a price without knowing all the specs?

Send the artwork, garment type and rough quantity. The quote comes back with construction suggestions and tiered pricing you can react to.
