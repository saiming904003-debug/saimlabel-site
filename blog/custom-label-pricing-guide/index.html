Custom Label Pricing: What Determines the Price of Your Quote

By the SAIM Label factory team · Updated 2026-08-14 · 7 min read

Short answer: The price of a custom label is decided by material and weave construction, finished size, quantity, thread colors, fold or finishing, and artwork complexity. Because every order is made to the design, factories quote after reviewing your artwork — not from a fixed price list.

In this guide
  1. Why there is no price list for custom labels
  2. What actually drives the price
  3. Why the quote needs your artwork
  4. How to get an accurate quote fast

Why there is no price list for custom labels

Woven labels, patches and care labels are made to order. The same nominal product — say, a neck label — can be woven in damask or satin, in 2 or 12 thread colors, folded three ways, laser-cut or die-cut, on cotton or recycled yarn. Each combination has a different machine setup, thread consumption and finishing time. A price list that claims one number for "woven labels" would be wrong for almost every real order. That is why honest factories quote per job: the design and material decide the cost, and the quote tells you exactly what your version costs.

Why the quote needs your artwork

Three things are only visible in the design. First, the weave density: a logo with 1.5 pt details needs high-density weaving and a different machine setup than a bold wordmark. Second, the color map: each thread color is a separate setup pass. Third, the fold allowance: the factory computes the cut size from the finished size plus the fold, and that changes yarn consumption. Without artwork, the factory would be quoting blind. That is why the standard flow is artwork first, ballpark range for planning, then a binding quote after review — followed by a proof drawing and physical sample before production. How to get an accurate quote fast

  • Vector artwork — AI, PDF, SVG, CDR or EPS at true size beats a screenshot every time.
  • Finished size — name the visible size in mm (or use the size chart to pick a starting point).
  • Quantity — a range is fine; per-piece pricing tiers make it easy to compare.
  • Application — garment type and position (collar, side seam, hat band) decide material and fold.
  • Deadline — production slots differ, so a target date avoids surprises.

Quotes are returned within one business day, and the proof drawing confirms size, colors and fold before anything is produced. Every quote line item shows what the price is made of — so when something changes, you can see exactly what moved.

FAQ

Can I get a price without sending artwork?

Not an accurate one. Material, weave density, size and finishing are all decided by the design, so a real quote needs your artwork first. We can give a ballpark range for a product type, but the binding price comes after artwork review.

What is the minimum order quantity?

For main products such as woven labels, woven patches and keychains, MOQ starts from 50 pieces. Your exact MOQ depends on the construction and finishing chosen.

Does a larger quantity lower the price per piece?

Yes. Setup and sampling costs are fixed, so they spread thinner over bigger runs, and thread waste per piece falls. Most buyers see the best per-piece price between 1,000 and 10,000 pieces.

How long does a quote take?

With artwork in hand, quotes are normally returned within one business day. The physical sample and proof drawing follow, and production starts after you confirm.

Why is my second quote higher than the first?

The usual causes are a change in material or weave construction, more thread colors, a bigger size, or an extra finishing step such as a merrow border or die-cut shape. Every change has a cost — the quote lists each line so you can see what moved.
