MOQ and Sampling Explained: How Trim Orders Actually Work

By the SAIM Label factory team · Updated 2026-07-02 · 5 min read

Short answer: MOQ exists because every design needs machine setup — loom threading, embroidery digitizing, or a PVC mold — that costs the same whether you order 50 or 5,000. MOQ counts per design and size version. At SAIM Label many catalog items start from 50 pieces, sampling is agreed in the quote, and approved repeat designs skip sampling entirely.

Why MOQ exists at all

A woven label run means threading a loom to your artwork; an embroidered patch means digitizing the stitch file; a PVC patch means cutting a mold. That setup cost is identical whether the run is 50 pieces or 5,000 — which is why per-unit prices fall so steeply with quantity, and why there is a floor below which a run stops making sense for both sides. MOQ is that floor, not a negotiation tactic.

How MOQ is counted

  • Per design — each artwork is its own setup, so two logos at 100 pieces each are two runs, not one 200-piece run.
  • Per size / color version — usually counted separately when the setup changes; size runs of the same label design are the friendly exception, produced in one run with a size breakdown.
  • Real numbers beat rules — our sample gallery shows the actual MOQ on every one of the 88 catalog photos; many start at 50 pieces and some lower.

Proofs, samples and when each applies

Two different checkpoints get called "sample". The digital proof — a production drawing with final size, colors and construction — is standard on every order and catches most issues. The pre-production sample — a physical piece made before bulk — applies when hand feel, color accuracy or a new construction matters. Sampling arrangements and costs are stated in the quote rather than assumed; and once a design is approved, repeat orders skip sampling because the settings, files and molds stay on file.

Use quantity tiers to see the curve

Ask for two or three quantities in one RFQ — 100 / 500 / 2,000 — and the quote shows how steeply your design's unit price falls. That one habit turns MOQ from a wall into a planning tool: sometimes 500 pieces cost only marginally more than 300, and the tier view is how you find out before committing.

FAQ

What is the MOQ for woven labels?

It depends on construction and size; many catalog items run from 50 pieces. The gallery shows real MOQs per item, and your artwork gets an exact number in the quote.

Do samples cost money?

Digital proofs are part of every order. Physical pre-production samples are arranged and priced in the quote — the answer depends on construction and whether a mold or digitizing is involved.

Can I order below the stated MOQ?

Sometimes, at a setup-weighted price. Ask with the artwork — some constructions flex further than others, as the low MOQs in the gallery show.

Why are repeat orders faster?

Approved artwork, machine settings, stitch files and molds are kept on file, so repeats go straight to production without sampling.
