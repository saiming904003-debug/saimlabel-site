Patch Backing and Border Guide: Iron-On, Velcro, Merrow, Laser

By the SAIM Label factory team · Updated 2026-07-02 · 6 min read

Short answer: Backing decides how a patch attaches; border decides how its edge looks and survives. Sew-on is the most durable backing, iron-on is convenient but must be heat-tested on the final fabric, hook-and-loop suits removable uniform patches. Merrow borders fit regular shapes; laser cutting handles irregular silhouettes.

In this guide
  1. Backings: how the patch attaches
  2. Borders: how the edge is finished
  3. Combinations that work

Backings: how the patch attaches

BackingBest forThe honest caveat
Sew-onGarments, bags, anything washed oftenNeeds a sewing step; most secure by far
Iron-on (heat seal)Retail packs, DIY application, light garmentsMust be heat-press tested on the final fabric; not for PVC patches
Hook-and-loopUniforms, tactical gear, rotating name patchesAdds thickness; loop side needs a home on the garment
AdhesiveEvents, packaging, short-term placementTemporary by design — not a sewing substitute
Pin / magnetBadges worn on formalwearDiscussed per order; magnet adds weight

Borders: how the edge is finished

BorderLookBest for
Merrow (overlock)Classic raised wrapped edgeRounds, rectangles, ovals, shields — regular shapes
Laser cutClean flat edge on any silhouetteIrregular shapes, modern logos, fine outlines
Heat cutEconomical sealed edgeBulk woven patches with simple forms
Satin stitchStitched outline inside the edgeDesigns that want a controlled drawn border

Combinations that work

  • Uniform name patch — embroidered, merrow border, hook-and-loop backing.
  • Streetwear logo patch — woven, laser-cut silhouette, sew-on.
  • Retail iron-on pack — woven or embroidered, heat-cut edge, iron-on backing with application instructions printed on the pack.
  • Outdoor emblem — PVC with molded sew channel or hook-and-loop; iron-on is not possible on PVC.

The pairing rule: pick the border by the artwork silhouette, pick the backing by how the end user attaches it — then say both in the RFQ.

FAQ

What is the most durable patch backing?

Sew-on. It survives industrial washing and daily wear better than any adhesive route, which is why uniforms and workwear standardize on it.

Do iron-on patches survive washing?

Yes when applied at the right time, temperature and pressure for that fabric — which is why a heat-press test on the final garment comes before bulk application.

Does hook-and-loop come with both sides?

The hook side is standard on the patch back; a matching loop piece for the garment can be included on request — state it in the RFQ.

Can a merrow border go on a star-shaped patch?

Sharp inner corners fight the overlock machine. Irregular silhouettes are better served by laser cutting, with satin stitch as the drawn-border alternative.
